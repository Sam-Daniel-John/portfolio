I’m Sam Daniel John Bosco, a Cybersecurity Master’s student who looks at security from two angles: the system and the human behind it. I’m particularly interested in the defensive side of cybersecurity, especially detecting, analyzing, and responding to real-world attacks. At the same time, my academic work has led me to explore how user behavior, decisions, and misunderstandings can also shape security outcomes. Combining technical security foundations with user-centered thinking helps me understand not only how security incidents happen, but also why they happen. I’m now looking to grow this perspective through hands-on security work, research, and practice.
